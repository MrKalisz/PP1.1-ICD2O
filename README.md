# GitHub Account Setup Guide for TDSB Students

## Important Requirements
- You must use your TDSB email address (ending in @tdsb.on.ca)
- Your username must follow this format: firstname-lastname-coursecode-sectionnumber
  - Example: john-smith-ics4u1-02

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Go to GitHub's Sign Up Page
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to https://github.com/signup
3. You should see the "Create your account" page

### 2. Enter Your Account Details
1. In the "Enter your email" field:
   - Type your TDSB email address (yourname@tdsb.on.ca)
   - Do NOT use any other email address

2. Create your username:
   - Follow the format: firstname-lastname-coursecode-sectionnumber
   - Use all lowercase letters
   - Include hyphens between each part
   - Double-check that you've included your section number

3. Create a strong password:
   - Must be at least 8 characters long
   - Include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
   - Store this password safely - you'll need it later

### 3. Verify Your Account
1. Complete the verification puzzle if prompted
2. Click "Create account"
3. Check your TDSB email for a verification code
4. Enter the code on GitHub to verify your email

### 4. Customize Your Experience
1. Answer the questions about your programming experience
2. Select any areas of interest
3. Choose whether you want to receive GitHub updates and announcements

### 5. Complete Your Profile
1. Add a profile picture (optional but recommended)
2. Fill in your bio with your current course information
3. Set your account visibility preferences

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### If You Can't Access Your TDSB Email
- Contact your teacher or IT support for assistance
- Make sure you're typing your email correctly

### If Your Username Is Already Taken
- Add your student number at the end
- Contact your teacher for alternative username formats

### If You Need Help
- Ask your teacher for assistance
- Visit GitHub's help center at https://docs.github.com/en
- Contact your school's IT support

## Important Notes
- Keep your login credentials secure
- Enable two-factor authentication for added security
- Bookmark the GitHub login page for easy access
- Remember to log out when using shared computers
